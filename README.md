# 🧬 QuantumBio — AI & Quantum Biomedical Intelligence Platform

**QuantumBio** is an AI- and quantum-enabled biomedical platform that integrates clinical diagnostics, radiology, biomarkers, genomics, medical search, patient profiling, and personalized therapeutic insights into a unified healthcare ecosystem.

> **Bridging Artificial Intelligence, Quantum Computing, and Biomedical Science.**

---

## 🚀 Overview

QuantumBio explores how **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** and **Quantum Computing concepts** can be combined with biomedical data to create an intelligent clinical analysis platform.

The platform brings together multiple healthcare domains including:

- 🩻 AI-assisted Radiology & DICOM Analysis
- 🧪 Clinical Biomarker Analysis
- 🧬 Pharmacogenomics & Epigenetics
- ⚛️ Quantum Energy & Biophysics Analysis
- 🔎 AI-Powered Biomedical Search
- 🧠 AI Clinical Diagnostics
- 👤 Patient Health Profiles
- 🧘 Therapeutic Yoga & Somatic Protocols
- 📊 Clinical History & Health Analytics
- 📄 Automated Clinical Reports

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🩻 Radiology & DICOM Analysis

Analyze medical imaging studies such as **MRI, CT, and X-Ray** with an interactive radiology workstation.

Features include:

- DICOM-based image visualization
- AI-assisted abnormality detection
- Detection bounding regions
- Confidence scores
- Severity classification
- Structured radiology impressions
- Clinical interpretation

---

### 🧪 Clinical Biomarker Analyzer

Analyze clinical biomarkers and identify potentially abnormal physiological patterns.

The system provides:

- Inflammatory biomarker analysis
- Cytokine profiling
- Metabolic indicators
- Neuro-endocrine analysis
- Autonomic stress assessment
- Reference-range comparison
- Interactive biomarker visualization

---

### 🧬 Pharmacogenomics & Epigenetics

Analyze genetic variants and explore their relationship with drug metabolism and personalized healthcare.

The module includes:

- SNP analysis
- Genotype interpretation
- Pharmacogenomic insights
- Drug-metabolism analysis
- Genetic risk indicators
- Epigenetic and nutrigenomic insights

---

### ⚛️ Quantum Biomedical Analysis

QuantumBio explores the application of quantum computational concepts to biomedical and molecular analysis.

The Quantum Energy module provides:

- Hartree energy visualization
- Quantum energy trajectories
- Ground-state analysis
- Coherence indicators
- Longitudinal energy tracking
- Biophysical trajectory visualization

The platform demonstrates an experimental approach toward combining **quantum computational models with biomedical intelligence**.

---

### 🔎 AI Biomedical Search

QuantumBio includes an AI-powered biomedical search interface for retrieving evidence and information from external medical sources.

It can be used for queries related to:

- Biomedical research
- Clinical studies
- Medical literature
- Treatment evidence
- Drug information
- Clinical trials
- Regulatory information

The system uses **AI-assisted search grounding** to provide more context-aware biomedical responses.

---

### 🧠 AI Clinical Diagnostic Engine

The diagnostic module combines patient information and AI-based analysis to generate structured clinical insights.

Users can provide:

- Anatomical region
- Symptoms
- Severity
- Chronicity
- Occupational/ergonomic context

The platform then generates an exploratory analysis with potential clinical observations and supportive therapeutic recommendations.

---

### 🧘 Therapeutic Yoga & Somatic Protocols

QuantumBio includes a structured therapeutic yoga library connecting specific practices with anatomical regions and physiological objectives.

The library includes protocols for:

- Cervical spine
- Lumbar spine
- Thoracic region
- Cranial & neural systems
- Scapulohumeral complex
- Abdominal and visceral systems

Each protocol can include breathing patterns, duration, anatomical mapping, and therapeutic intent.

---

### 👤 Patient Health Profile

The Patient Health Profile provides a centralized view of longitudinal health information.

It can display:

- Diagnostic history
- Biomarker trends
- Quantum energy graphs
- HRV-related metrics
- Somatic compliance
- Previous clinical observations
- Therapeutic protocols
- AI-generated health insights
